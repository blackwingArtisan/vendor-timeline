QUILLFEATHER PHOTO STUDIO
Wedding Photography Agreement

This Agreement is made on October 12, 2026 between Quillfeather Photo Studio ("Photographer") and Maya Okafor and Jonas Lindqvist ("Clients").

1. Event. Photographer will photograph the Clients' wedding on June 12, 2027 (the "Event Date") at Thistlewick Barn & Gardens in Alder Falls.

2. Coverage. Coverage includes eight hours on the Event Date with two photographers, an online gallery of at least 500 edited images, and one 10x10 linen album.

3. Fee. The total fee for the services in this Agreement is $4,800.00 (the "Total Fee").

4. Retainer. A non-refundable retainer of 25% of the Total Fee is due on or before December 18, 2026 to reserve the Event Date.

5. Balance. The remaining balance of $3,600.00 is due thirty (30) days before the Event Date.

6. Payment. Please send all payments by PayPal to billing@quillfeather-photo.example.com.

7. Late payment. If the retainer is not received by its due date, Photographer may release the Event Date to another client.

8. Delivery. The gallery will be delivered within eight weeks of the Event Date.

Signed by Quillfeather Photo Studio, Maya Okafor and Jonas Lindqvist.
